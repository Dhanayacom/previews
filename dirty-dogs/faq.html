FAQ

What are functional mushrooms?

Mushrooms grown for more than dinner, like Turkey Tail and Lion's Mane. "Functional" is a common label, not a science term. Ours are farm-grown.

Why is there no seal or vet badge on the bag?

Because we haven't earned one yet. When we do, it goes here with the proof. [testing, quality-seal or vet-review status]

Is there xylitol in any chew?

No. It's toxic to dogs, and it hides in some peanut butters, gum and human gummies. Check the label on anything you share.

My dog is pregnant, nursing or still a puppy. Can they have it?

Ask your vet first. Safe use in pregnant or breeding dogs hasn't been shown. [minimum age]

What if my dog takes daily medicine?

Check with your vet before you start, and bring the ingredient list. Your vet knows your dog's whole story.

What if my dog gets into the whole bag?

Call your vet or ASPCA Poison Control at (888) 426-4435 right away. Then store the bag up high with the lid shut. Soft chews smell like treats, and some dogs are clever.

How should I store the chews?

[storage guidance from manufacturer]. Close the lid tight and keep the bag out of reach of children and pets.

Can my cat have one?

Not yet. Dirty Dogs chews are made for dogs only, and cats have different needs.

No. FDA doesn't approve chews like these before they're sold. That's one reason we keep our claims modest and our sources public.
