What's studied in dogs, and what isn't

Studied

A few small, short trials in healthy beagles. Reishi was eaten well and didn't upset digestion. A blend of Turkey Tail, Reishi, Lion's Mane and colostrum was well tolerated and shifted gut bacteria a little, with no immune benefit found.

Not studied

Our chews, or Lion's Mane for memory in dogs. Most online buzz comes from people, mice and lab dishes. So we keep our promises small.

FAQ

Are mushrooms safe for dogs?

Wild ones can be deadly, so never let your dog snack on the trail. Ours are cultivated on a farm, then made into a chew. Ask your vet first, especially if your dog takes medicine or has a health condition.

Which chew should I pick?

Daily Dig suits most healthy adult dogs. Old Soul is for seniors. Belly Rub is for dogs whose digestion needs a daily routine. Still stuck? Email us and we'll help you choose. [senior age guidance]

How much do I give?

Check the weight chart on each chew's page. Give it with or after a meal. If your vet tells you something different, go with your vet.

When will I notice a difference?

We won't promise a timeline or a result. Think of it like a daily walk, a habit that adds up. If your dog's health changes, stop the chews and call your vet.

What if my dog won't eat it?

Then it's on us. Reach out within the guarantee window and we'll refund you in full. [return terms]
