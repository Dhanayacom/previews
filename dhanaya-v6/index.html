Quick answers

Is Cash on Delivery available?

Yes, Cash on Delivery is open wherever your pincode allows. You can also pay online by UPI or card.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery takes about 2 days on average, and some pincodes can take a little longer. It’s free across India, and there’s no delivery on Sundays or holidays.

What is the 7-day Magic Promise?

If you’re not happy with your set, even after your child has used it, WhatsApp us within 7 days of delivery. We arrange the pickup and replace it or refund you.

What’s in the set, and what age is it for?

You get 5 books (Numbers, Maths, English, Drawing and Hindi) and the magic pen. The set is made for ages 3+.
