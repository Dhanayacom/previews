Returns, replacements and refunds

Our 7-day Magic Promise covers used books too. If you’re not happy with your set, or anything arrives damaged, WhatsApp us within 7 days of delivery. We arrange the pickup and replace it or refund you.

How to raise it

  1. WhatsApp us within 7 days of delivery with your order number. If something is damaged, add a photo.
  2. We reply on WhatsApp and set up a pickup from your address.
  3. Once the set is picked up, we replace it or refund you. To be added

What qualifies

  • You’re not happy with the set, even if your child has used the books.
  • The ink does not fade, or the pen doesn’t work.
  • Anything in the set arrives damaged.
  • Your request reaches us within 7 days of delivery.

Refund timing

Once your refund is approved, it takes 5 to 7 working days. Paid by Cash on Delivery? We refund to your UPI ID or bank account.
