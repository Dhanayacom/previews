Questions parents ask

Here are short answers about the Dhanaya Magic Copybook set, covering the 5 books, what’s inside, how the ink fades, refills, safety, Cash on Delivery, delivery times and returns. If your question isn’t here, WhatsApp us and we’ll help.

The product

What comes in the set?

You get 5 books (Numbers, Maths, English, Drawing and Hindi) and the magic pen with ink that fades, plus a grip and refills, all packed together in one box.

Does the ink really fade?

Yes. The ink fades on its own after your child writes, so the same page is ready to use again. Watching the ink fade is the fun part.

Can the same page be used again?

Yes. Once the ink fades, your child can trace the same page again, so you don’t need to buy a new book every time a page is finished.

How big are the books?

We’ll add the exact size here once it’s checked on the printed books.

What language are the books in?

The set has an English book and a Hindi book. The Hindi book covers all 49 letters, from अ to ज्ञ, each with a picture word.

Where is it made?

The set is made in China and assembled in India.

Using it

What age is it for? Can a 2.5 year old use it?

The set is made for children aged 3 and up. If you want to try it with a 2.5 year old, please sit with them the whole time and keep small parts like the pen cap out of reach.

How long should my child use it each day?

We suggest about 10 minutes a day, because short sessions keep it fun. It helps to pick a fixed time, like after breakfast, before school or during a long car ride.

Will it work for a left-handed child?

The pen and the grip can be held in either hand, so a left-handed child can trace the grooves too.

What if my child presses too hard?

Show your child a light touch, because the pen only needs to glide along the groove and does not need any force.

What is the grip for?

The grip slides onto the pen and gives small fingers a place to rest while they write. Put it on the pen before your child starts.

Refills

How long do the refills last?

That depends on how much your child writes each day. Refills come in the box, and when the ink starts to look light, you simply put in a fresh one.

Do I need to buy refills separately?

No. Every set comes with its refills in the box, and refills are not sold separately.

The set is made for children aged 3 and up. Please sit with young children while they use it, and keep the pen cap and other small parts away from babies.
