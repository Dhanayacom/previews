Questions, answered plainly

1. What is snow fungus?
It's Tremella fuciformis, a white, frilly mushroom that has long been part of Chinese soups and sweets.
2. Why only one mushroom?
Others, like reishi and chaga, carry safety cautions, so we left them out. So we kept just one: snow fungus, a mushroom with a long history in Chinese soups and sweets, and a short label.
3. Why hyaluronic acid?
It holds on to water, so it's a natural fit for a gummy made to support skin hydration.* Want the full label? It's all on our Ingredients page.
4. Is there anything from animals in it?
No, the gummy is set with plant pectin, and every ingredient is vegan.
5. Is there biotin?
There's no added biotin. The FDA warns that large amounts of biotin can throw off some lab tests, so we kept it out.
6. When will I notice a difference?
We don't promise a timeline, because everyone's skin is different. Take it daily and notice how your skin feels to you.
7. Who is it for?
It's for adults of any gender who want one simple daily step for their skin.
8. Is it safe with my medication?
Ask your doctor before you start, especially if you take medication or are pregnant or nursing.
