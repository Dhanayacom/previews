What's inside

Three ingredients, labeled honestly

i.Mushroom

Snow fungus (Tremella)

A white, frilly mushroom used in Chinese cooking for centuries. It's our story ingredient, picked for its long kitchen history. We make no skin claim for this one.

ii.Hydrate

Hyaluronic acid

Your body already makes it, and it loves water. In every chew, it's here to support skin hydration from within, so skin feels soft.*

iii.Skin barrier

Konjac ceramides

Ceramides are fats that help skin hold water, and ours come from konjac. Think of it as the lid on the jar. Helps maintain your skin's natural moisture barrier.*

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly

Is the Snow Gummy vegan?
Yes, it's set with pectin, a fiber that comes from fruit, and nothing in it comes from animals.
Does it replace my skincare?
No. Keep your cleanser and your sunscreen, and add the gummy as one extra step that takes a single chew.
So why is snow fungus in it?
It's our tradition ingredient, a mushroom with a long history in Chinese kitchens. No skin claim for it, as that job goes to hyaluronic acid and ceramides.
How much sugar is in it?
Each gummy has TBC g of sugar. It's printed on the label, so you never have to guess.
Can I take it if I'm pregnant, nursing or on medication?
Please talk to your doctor first, as you would before adding anything new to your day.
