What's studied, what's tradition

Here's what we lean on, and how strong it is. These studies tested ingredients, not the Snow Gummy itself.

Studied

Hyaluronic acid

In one trial, 40 healthy adults took hyaluronic acid or a dummy pill by mouth. The hyaluronic acid group had more water in the top layer of skin.(1) A review of 7 small trials found similar gains in hydration, and its authors note the trials were small and varied.(2) Most of these trials were co-written by people from the company that makes the ingredient.

Studied, early

Konjac ceramides

In one trial of 51 adults, skin was rated less dry with konjac ceramides than with a dummy pill.(3) It is one small study, and it was registered after it began.

Tradition

Snow fungus

Nobody has yet run a human trial of eating snow fungus for skin. One small study put a gel with snow fungus on people's hands, which tells us nothing about eating it.(4) A 2023 review says most research so far is in cells and animals.(5) It's in the gummy for its long history, and the skin claims rest on the other two.
