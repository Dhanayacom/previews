FAQ

Questions, answered plainly

What does it taste like?
It tastes like ripe white peach, with a jelly-soft chew.
When should I take it?
Any time works. Pair it with something you already do every day, like your morning coffee or brushing your teeth.
Can I take two for more?
No, because one gummy is a full serving. Please keep to one a day, as the label says.
What if I miss a day?
If you miss a day, just take your next gummy tomorrow as usual, and please don't double up to catch up.
